## About Me

I enjoy turning ideas into working systems — from **computer-vision and gesture-based applications** to **deep-learning models, intelligent automation, and algorithmic problem solving**. My work reflects an interest in understanding not only how to use AI tools, but also the underlying algorithms, mathematics, and engineering that make them work.

My current toolkit includes **Python, C/C++, TensorFlow/Keras, OpenCV, MediaPipe, scikit-learn, Git, and GitHub**, with hands-on experience experimenting with CNNs, real-time vision systems, gesture recognition, machine-learning models, and embedded/robotics projects.

Beyond individual projects, I regularly work through **data structures and algorithms**, using C/C++ to strengthen my problem-solving fundamentals.

I'm currently exploring deeper areas of **Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, AI applications, and intelligent software systems** — with a focus on learning by building, experimenting, debugging, and improving.

> **Understand the mathematics. Build the system. Break it. Debug it. Make it better.**
